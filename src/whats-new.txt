Desk Console 1.8.5 -- what's new since 1.7.1

1.8.4 + 1.8.5: LESS ON THE REMOTE PCS' TASKBARS, AND UNATTENDED UPDATES
- Every PC now updates Desk Console by itself, within about 2 minutes of a new version reaching the shared folder -- even where nobody opens Desk Console. No more restarting it on each PC.
- Deskflow now moves to the notification area on every PC, including those whose Deskflow used the machine-wide settings (1.8.1 skipped them). Nothing to do.
- Every PC now hides RustDesk's "someone is connected" window -- the taskbar button that shows while you control it; RustDesk's tray icon stays. Someone sitting at a PC can then no longer see that it is being controlled. To keep the window on a PC, untick its card's Actions > "Hide RustDesk's connection window there".

1.8.2 + 1.8.3: EVERY PC ON ITS OWN DISPLAY, OR TABS -- YOUR CHOICE
- A second PC opened as a tab of the first one's window instead of on its own display. Each PC now opens in its own window, on the display that matches its place in the desk layout.
- Prefer one window with a tab per PC? Untick "Each PC in its own window" in Desk Console's toolbar (or in the tray icon menu).
- If RustDesk sessions are open when you update, close them once: the change applies to the next View + control after that.

1.8.1: DESKFLOW STAYS IN THE NOTIFICATION AREA
- Deskflow no longer opens its window at sign-in or sits on the taskbar: it starts hidden, closing its window keeps it running, and its tray icon opens it.
- Nothing to do: within a few minutes of this update, each PC's background task switches it over once (Deskflow's window restarts; the keyboard and mouse stay connected). Change it afterwards in Deskflow's own settings and it stays changed.

1.8.0: EACH PC OPENS ON ITS OWN DISPLAY
- View + control (and Open all) put a PC's RustDesk window on the display that matches its place in your desk layout: with the server in the centre, a PC on its left opens on the left display and one on its right opens on the right. With no display in that direction, or a single display, the window opens normally and you can move it.
- Optional: tray icon menu > "Hide RustDesk sessions from the taskbar" (off by default). Open RustDesk sessions are then listed in the tray menu: click one to bring it back.
